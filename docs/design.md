# Design record

The decisions that shaped the proof of concept, in the order taken, as the transparency policy of the journal asks.

1. **The unit is the single firm.** The instrument moved from aggregate innovation rates to reference organizations that read a firm's own record, so that theory weights can be calibrated on one firm's early decisions and validated on its later ones.
2. **Two windows around the pandemic.** Products launched in 2018 and 2019 are decided in 2019; products launched in 2023 and 2024 are decided in 2024 and 2025. The archive holds no usable pages for 2020 and 2021, so the 2022 decisions about the first window fall across a gap and are not compared with the record; they serve only as the second year of the own-history run.
3. **Measurement from the archive alone.** Products, prominence, statements, strategy, situation, and the NACE division are coded from archived pages under written protocols, each with a verbatim quote. The division read from the website replaced the division of the sample frame, which removed the last proprietary input from the analysis.
4. **A probability instead of a decision.** The agent states the probability that the firm withdraws a product within a year. An earlier elicitation of a decision and a confidence inflated the withdrawal rates and was dropped.
5. **Lapses are not withdrawals.** A product that returns after a year without archive is not counted as withdrawn, and silence about a product is shown to the agent as nothing reported.
6. **Theories as texts in two framings.** Each theory enters as a short text, in a considerations framing that lists what management weighs and a conditions framing that states when it withdraws; the considerations framing is the main one.
7. **Anchors tested by removal.** Every theory is run without the web anchors and without the anchors of its own constructs, which tests whether a theory acts through the anchors its text names.
8. **The own-history run on the same product.** The second decision year is simulated with the agent's own first-year decisions as its past, drawn ten times from its probabilities, with the firm's actual decisions as a control. The hot stove effect is read on the same product, as the persistence of a product the agent ruled for and the hardening of an early doubt, after a first version that measured the withdrawal of other products.
9. **Calibration before the pandemic, validation after it.** Weights are fitted on the 2019 decisions and tested on the held-out decisions of 2024 and 2025, by level and by ranking against a regression on the product record.
10. **Three models, one reference.** Opus 5.5 is the reference model; gpt-5.4 and DeepSeek V4 Pro repeat every run, so that each result can be checked across models.
