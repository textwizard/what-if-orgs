# Anchors of the theories

Each theory of the reference organizations weighs a few constructs when management decides whether to keep or drop a
new product. The anchors give the agent evidence about these constructs for its firm and its products, so that its
reasoning can combine the theory text with the situation of the firm. Every anchor is observed before the decision it
informs. The table names the construct, the indicator on the website or in open statistics, and the protocol that
codes it.

| Theory | Construct | Indicator | Level | Coded by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Behavioral theory of the firm | Performance against aspirations | Results stated for the product, such as sales, customers, or awards | product, year | year.md |
| | Performance of comparable firms | Change of turnover in the industry | industry | Eurostat |
| | Performance of the firm | Growth or difficulties reported by the firm | firm | context.md |
| | Organisational slack | Investment, hiring, expansion, size | firm | context.md, frame |
| Absorptive capacity | Prior related knowledge | Research and development of its own | firm | context.md |
| | Relatedness of the product | Closeness of the product to the established offer | product | relatedness.md |
| | Access to external knowledge | Research cooperation, EU research projects | firm | context.md, CORDIS |
| | Knowledge intensity of the industry | Shares of in-house R&D, patents, and cooperation with universities | industry | CIS |
| Escalation of commitment | Investment in the product | Investment dedicated to the product, such as a plant, line, or team | product, year | year.md |
| | Public commitment | Announcement of the product in news or press, and its place on the home page at launch | product, year | year.md, prominence |
| | Centrality | The product presented as a flagship or a focus of the firm's direction | product, year | year.md |
| Real options reasoning | Uncertainty of the prospects | Novelty of the product, a pilot or test stage, distance from the established offer | product | year.md, relatedness.md |
| | Cost of keeping the option | Scale of the commitment, such as a single model or a full product line | product | year.md |
| | Other options held | Number of products offered and of new products launched | firm | products |

The theory of value of the firm, coded in 2017 and 2022 (value.md), is not shown to the agent. It serves the check of
coherence between the calibrated weights and the way the firm describes how it creates value.
