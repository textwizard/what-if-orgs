The management of this firm approaches its decisions from the perspective of workplace facilities. Firms occupy offices, workshops, or warehouses that differ in size, layout, and location. Rooms are equipped with furniture, lighting, heating, and ventilation, and many buildings have kitchens, meeting rooms, and parking spaces for staff and visitors. Facilities are cleaned, inspected, and maintained by internal staff or external service providers. Floor plans record the use of each room, and signs guide visitors through the building. Visitors register at reception, and deliveries are received at a separate entrance. Safety equipment such as fire extinguishers and first-aid kits is placed according to building regulations, and keys or access cards regulate who may enter which areas.
