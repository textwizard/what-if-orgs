When deciding about the products of this firm, reason from the perspective of the Absorptive Capacity framework. The framework holds that a firm's ability to recognise the value of new information, to assimilate it, and to apply it commercially depends on the related knowledge it already holds. Knowledge accumulates through the firm's own activities and experiences and forms the base on which later learning builds. This creates path dependence, since firms with a broad and related knowledge base learn more readily from new information than firms with little related knowledge. The framework distinguishes the capacity to acquire and understand knowledge from the capacity to transform and exploit it, with internal integration linking the two.
