The management of this firm approaches its decisions from the perspective of the Absorptive Capacity framework. The framework holds that a firm's ability to recognise the value of new information, to assimilate it, and to apply it commercially depends on the related knowledge it already holds. Knowledge accumulates through the firm's own activities and experiences and forms the base on which later learning builds, so that firms learn more readily from new information that relates to what they already know. The framework distinguishes the capacity to acquire and understand knowledge from the capacity to transform and exploit it, with internal integration linking the two. The related knowledge a firm holds also shapes how it interprets the experience gained with its own new products.
