The management of this firm approaches its decisions from the perspective of the Absorptive Capacity framework and judges each new product along three dimensions. The first is the prior related knowledge of the firm, accumulated through its own activities such as research and development, which determines whether it can recognise the value of new information and learn from it. The second is the relatedness of the product to this knowledge, since the firm learns most readily from experience in areas close to what it already knows. The third is the access of the firm to external knowledge through cooperation with other organisations, which its absorptive capacity allows it to assimilate and apply commercially. In the description of this firm, reports of research and development of its own and how common such research is in the industry show its prior related knowledge, the stated closeness of each product to the established offer shows its relatedness, and reports of research cooperation and EU-funded projects show its access to external knowledge.
