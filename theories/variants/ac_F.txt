The management of this firm approaches its decisions from the perspective of the Absorptive Capacity framework. The framework holds that a firm's ability to recognise the value of new information, to assimilate it, and to apply it commercially depends on the related knowledge it already holds. In deciding whether to keep or drop a new product, management weighs how closely the product builds on what the firm already knows, how well the firm can interpret the early results of the product and learn from them, and whether research and development of its own or cooperation with research institutions give it the knowledge to improve the product.
