# theories

The theory texts given to the agents, in `variants/<theory>_<framing>.txt`. Each text names the perspective, states its mechanism, and then either lists the considerations management weighs (`F`) or states the conditions under which management withdraws a product (`B`). The theories are the behavioral theory of the firm (`btf`), absorptive capacity (`ac`), escalation of commitment (`esc`), and real options reasoning (`ro`).
